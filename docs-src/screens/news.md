# News

- **News** (Pi): the Pi service reads the RSS feeds of RTP, Expresso, Publico and CNN Portugal (Sabado is switched off for now), Aviacao TV and the F1 sources every 10 minutes (conditional requests, so an unchanged feed is not downloaded again) and serves `news.json` with the headlines of the last 12 hours. The same story from several sources is shown once (headlines that share enough words, within 6 hours). **Breaking**: the source marks the headline as "Ultima hora" or "Urgente", in the last 2 hours. **Top story**: the same story in 3 or more of the general sources in the last 3 hours. Both come first. Opinion, podcasts and match reports are left out. Three headlines per page (a headline whose source gives a picture shows it small, 96 x 72, on the right; the Pi service keeps only https pictures from the CDNs of RTP, Expresso, CNN Portugal, PlanetF1 and Aviacao TV, already small, and leaves out RTP's generic placeholders; a picture that fails to load is left out), up to four pages, in this order: the general headlines, then **Aviation News** (the 3 latest of Aviacao TV in the last 3 days, kept apart because it publishes about five a day and among the general headlines it was gone from the screen within half an hour), then F1 News; each of these two pages takes the place of a page of general headlines. The Star4000 fonts have no accents, so they are removed. Each headline and the bottom of the screen show the source by its logo (at the bottom, the general, aviation and F1 sources are parted by short vertical lines) (`images/news/`, from the owner's files; `utils/news.mjs`), on a small white plate (RTP, Expresso, Publico, CNN Portugal), a black one (FlyingLapF1, F1PT) or a red one in the red of Sabado's own box (Aviacao TV, whose logo is used here only as its plane symbol, without the words; Sabado when it is on), so all logos have the same margins and size. Expresso's programmes, interviews, podcasts and satire (Inimigo Publico) and Sabado's photo galleries are left out by their categories.
- **F1 News** (last page of News): the latest Formula 1 headlines of [FlyingLapF1](https://flyinglapf1.com) and [F1PT](https://www.f1pt.pt) (both Portuguese) and [PlanetF1](https://www.planetf1.com) (English, `https://www.planetf1.com/rss`, about ten a day), up to three from the last 7 days, at most two from each so no source takes all the places (the Pi service keeps two from each source, the screen then shows the three most recent); the same story in two sources is shown once. They are kept apart from the other sources so they are not joined to general stories; podcast episodes (items with audio) are left out. With F1 news the other headlines take at most three pages.
- **Bottom line**: with *Show Breaking News in the Bottom Line* on, each round of the bottom line ends with the breaking news (at most two), with a red BREAKING label (the source is only on the News screen): shown from the start for 3 seconds, then crawling slowly to the left, within the same margins as the weather lines, until the end has been read, and staying another 3.5 seconds. Without breaking news nothing is added.
- If the Pi service does not answer for a moment (a request gives up after 4 seconds), the last list is kept for up to 30 minutes, so the News screen and the breaking news do not disappear until the next refresh; after that the list is empty.
- Only headlines, with their source, are shown; nothing is stored on disk. A long headline uses smaller letters instead of being cut (20pt on two lines, 16pt on three, 14pt on four; the limits are lower beside a picture, whose space it takes), and the time is centred on the source logo.
