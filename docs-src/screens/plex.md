# Plex

- **Plex Now Playing** (Pi): off by default, in the cinema look of Plex Recommends (the title reads "Plex Now Playing"). One page of 15 seconds for each session of a movie or an episode that is playing or paused (music is left out), 3 at most. The Pi service answers `plexnow.json` from Tautulli's `get_activity` (kept for 15 seconds, and the posters, of the show for an episode, in memory only; the same Tautulli key and settings as Plex Recommends, nothing more to set up). The page asks once a minute and redraws only when a session starts, stops, pauses or resumes, so a film shows up within about a minute; with nothing playing, or without the Pi or Tautulli, the screen is skipped. The progress moves on from the time of the answer, and "Ends HH:mm" is left out while paused. The name of the user and the device are shown as Plex gives them. Turn it on with `plex-now-checkbox=true` in the TV address.
- **Plex Recommends** (Pi): two pages of 15 seconds, off by default. The title is "Plex Recommends" in one line on both: the Plex logo (supplied by the owner, `images/plex/plex-logo.svg`, its black parts made white to show on the velvet) in place of the word, then "Recommends". It looks like a cinema: red velvet on the header band and where the WeatherStar orange was (`BackGround1_1_cinema.png`, `recolor_theme.py cinema`), a dark auditorium for the box with a row of still marquee bulbs along its top (nothing is animated, for the Pi), a gold frame on the poster and gold and cream letters. Page 1: the movie added most recently to the Plex library. Page 2: a movie chosen at random from those with no plays, one that is not the latest and not the one shown before when there are others. In a season page 2 recommends only films from the list of that season, matched by title and year (one year more or less allowed), and no page 2 when the library has none: on 30 and 31 October (the Auto days of Halloween Effects) the Rotten Tomatoes "200 Best Horror Movies of All Time" and the films of the Plex genre Horror; from 1 to 25 December (the Auto days of Christmas Effects) the Rotten Tomatoes "100 Best Christmas Movies of All Time". The lists are in the Pi service, not in this repository. Each page has the poster on the left and, beside it, the title, year and runtime, the two Rotten Tomatoes scores, each with the picture of its verdict (the Tomatometer of the critics with a ripe or a rotten tomato, the audience score with upright or spilled popcorn; pictures supplied by the owner, in `images/plex/`; the verdict is the one Plex gives, or 60 % when it gives none; a score is left out when it is not from Rotten Tomatoes, never an IMDb or TMDb score under its name) and the synopsis, cut at a word where the box ends. The bottom bar has only the page counter (1/2): no source, it is Plex.
- **How it works**: the Pi service asks [Tautulli](https://tautulli.com) (on the Raspy5, connected to the Plex server) every 30 minutes (every 5 after a failure) for the latest movie (`get_recently_added`), the movies of the libraries with no plays (`get_library_media_info`), the details of the two chosen (`get_metadata`) and their posters (`pms_image_proxy`), and serves `plex.json` and the two posters (`plex-poster-latest.jpg`, `plex-poster-random.jpg`, kept in memory only). The screen is skipped when the service has nothing, which is also what happens anywhere but the Pi. "No plays" is what Tautulli reports for the library; a movie watched outside Plex, or on a server Tautulli does not follow, still counts as unwatched.
- **Setting it up** (in the Pi's `/etc/skypaddock-hub.conf`, never in this repository): `TAUTULLI_KEY` (the API key of Tautulli; without it the screen is off), `TAUTULLI_URL` (default `http://raspy5:8181`) and `TAUTULLI_SECTIONS` (the numbers of the movie libraries to use, separated by commas; empty means every movie library). The key is only sent to Tautulli, never to the page, and is left out of the errors and the log. The Pi warning line adds `Plex stale since ...` when the data is more than 3 hours old.
