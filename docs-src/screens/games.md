# Games

- **GTA VI on PC** (Pi, off by default): the title is on one line, the game's logo (`server/images/gta6-logo.svg`, supplied by the owner of this project) followed by "On PC". How close the community projects are to running the leaked Xbox build of GTA VI on a PC, in three pages of 10 seconds. **The leader**: name, score, graphics card, GitHub stars and commits of the week, the five steps of the ladder (Exe, Frame, Menu, World, Play: lit when reached, half lit when partly reached) and the four meters that fill the second half of the score once a build is playable (Story, Picture, Speed in fps, Stability). **The race**: the top five with a bar each, and the leader's score day by day as a line, with how many projects are in the race. **Latest news**: the last three headlines with date and category (the page is skipped when there are none). Every page shows at the top right whether the missing executable is out and for how many days. The data is the public tracker of [PortWatch VI](https://portwatch-vi.xyz): the Pi service (`hub_gta.py`) reads its `tracker.json` every 3 hours, works out each project's score with the same ladder as the tracker (10 points per step up to Playable at 50, then story, picture, speed and stability fill the other 50) and serves `gta.json` with the top five (score, ladder, meters, graphics card, stars, commits), the executable state, the leader's score of the last ten days and three headlines. The scores are PortWatch VI's estimates from public READMEs and commits, not ours; the data is third-party and partly written by AI, so only numbers and headlines are shown, never links, and control characters are removed. The screen is skipped away from the Pi and when the numbers are older than 12 hours. The colours follow the game's artwork, a Vice City sunset (violet, hot pink, orange): `_gta.scss` and the backgrounds `BackGround1_1_gta.png` / `BackGround1_1_wide_gta.png` (`recolor_theme.py gta`). The screen shows no source line, and it is the last screen of the rotation. Nothing about the game itself is hosted or linked.
