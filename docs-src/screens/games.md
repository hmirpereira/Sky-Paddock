# Games

- **GTA VI on PC** (Pi, off by default): the title is on one line, the game's logo (`server/images/gta6-logo.svg`, supplied by the owner of this project) followed by "On PC". How close the community projects are to running the leaked Xbox build of GTA VI on a PC. It shows the closest project with its name, score and a progress bar, the step it has reached (Exe runs, First frame, Main menu, In the world, Playable) and its frame rate, then the next three projects, whether the missing executable is out (and since when) and the latest headline. The data is the public tracker of [PortWatch VI](https://portwatch-vi.xyz): the Pi service (`hub_gta.py`) reads its `tracker.json` every 3 hours, works out each project's score with the same ladder as the tracker (10 points per step up to Playable at 50, then story, picture, speed and stability fill the other 50) and serves `gta.json` with the top four, the executable state and three headlines. The scores are PortWatch VI's estimates from public READMEs and commits, not ours; the data is third-party and partly written by AI, so only numbers and headlines are shown, never links, and control characters are removed. The screen is skipped away from the Pi and when the numbers are older than 12 hours. The colours follow the game's artwork, a Vice City sunset (violet, hot pink, orange): `_gta.scss` and the backgrounds `BackGround1_1_gta.png` / `BackGround1_1_wide_gta.png` (`recolor_theme.py gta`). The screen shows no source line, and it is the last screen of the rotation. Nothing about the game itself is hosted or linked.
