# Look and feel

- **The page** (around the screen; not shown on the TV): it follows the window. On a phone everything is in one column; from 1100 px the screen is on the left, as big as the window allows (`resize` in `navigation.mjs`), and the options are in a column of their own on the right that scrolls by itself, so they are always in view; from 1600 px that column is wider (`_site.scss`). The column has the location and last update on top (always in view) and four tabs, kept in this browser only (`tabs.mjs`): **Screens** (the screens in groups, in two columns), **Settings** (grouped as Location, Units, Screens, Look and Advanced; `settings.mjs`), **TV and link** (Copy TV command and permalink) and **About**.
- **Scrolling**: a screen that scrolls (Hourly Forecast, Hazards, F1 Drivers, F1 Grid) scrolls its whole box, so the background stripes move with the text, as in the Hourly Forecast; the column headers or the race title stay at the top. This is the way for any new screen that scrolls: extend `ScrollWeatherDisplay` (`scrollweatherdisplay.mjs`), put the rows in and call `setScrollDistance` with the height that does not fit; the time on screen is 3 s still, the scroll at 50 px/s and 3 s still (`utils/scroll-timing.mjs`), so it follows the number of rows (the idea comes from the original WeatherStar 4000+, [ws4kp](https://github.com/netbymatt/ws4kp)). The whole scroll is one screen of the rotation, so the Next and Previous buttons skip the whole screen.
- **Bottom bar (sources and pages)**: where a screen shows its source (F1 Telemetry, Verstappen Jet, GT World) or only its update time and page counter (Departures, Cancellations and METAR) it is a line at the bottom of the box, always still, centred, on the background of the screen and at half opacity (`views/partials/footer.ejs`, `_source-footer.scss`, `utils/footer.mjs`). A screen with several pages shows the page counter (1/3) at the bottom right of that bar, never in the title. Only News has a bar of its own colour, because its sources are logos (left) and the counter is at the right. The map credit on the Overhead plan stays on the map, as the OpenStreetMap licence asks. On the aviation screens only the credits a source asks for stay on screen (adsb.fi and The OpenSky Network on the Verstappen Jet, the OpenStreetMap credit on the Overhead plan); the others (AeroDataBox, aviationweather.gov, VATSIM, OurAirports) are credited in the Credits section of the page and here.
- **Loading screen**: the list of screens with their state; with more than 11 it goes into two columns by group (Weather, Aviation, Personal, News, Formula 1), each with its title, so it always fits.
- **Countdown bar**: on the line between the main area and the bottom line, a thin coloured line that fills from left to right while a screen is on; when it is full the next screen comes. Yellow and orange on the weather screens, white and red on F1 and GT, light blue and amber on the aviation ones, with a light running along it. It stops while the rotation is paused. Screens that scroll (Hourly, Hazards, F1 Drivers, F1 Grid) have no bar, since the scrolling already shows how far they are. It moves in small steps (4 pixels at a time) so the Pi does not redraw it on every frame. Not on Radar and the loading screen, which have no bottom line.
- **Logo**: `images/skypaddock-logo.png` (340 x 268) in the header of every screen, `images/skypaddock-logo-large.png` (680 x 536) on the loading screen and `images/Logo192.png` as the browser and app icon, all made from the owner's high resolution drawing.
- **Themes**: recoloured copies of the WeatherStar backgrounds for the F1 screens (red and black, `_f1-theme.scss`) and the aviation screens (navy and silver, `_aviation-theme.scss`). Colours only; no Formula 1 or airline marks.
- **Icons**, drawn by the owner of this project: `images/airlines/<IATA code>.png` (airline tails, 128 x 128), `images/teams/<constructorId>.png` (F1 teams, 128 x 128) and `images/overhead-logos/<ICAO code>.png` (airline logos for the radar, 48 x 16, listed in `RADAR_LOGOS` in `overhead.mjs`; `LOGO_ALIASES` maps other callsign prefixes of the same brand). `images/flags/<country>.png` are 24 x 16 pixel flags scaled up without smoothing. A missing icon or flag is simply not shown.
- **Accents**: the Star4000 fonts draw accented letters blank, so accents are removed from names (F1 screens, Cancellations, Hazards).
- **Analogue look** (`_scan-lines.scss`), each a setting of its own and all static, so they cost the Pi almost nothing:
  - **Scan lines**: drawn in real pixels, one dark line of one device pixel every two, so they stay even at any scale: on the TV (kiosk mode) over the whole window (540 lines at 1080p), on the page over the screen only, as high as the scaled screen (not over the control bar). Drawn inside the 640 x 480 screen, as in the original, the scale made them uneven: 2 or 3 pixels thick on the TV (2.25x), 2 to 6 pixels apart on a desktop page (1.54x). On a screen with display scaling (125 %, for instance) a CSS pixel is not a device pixel, so the thickness is worked out from the pixel ratio (`--scan-px`, set by `resize()` in `navigation.mjs`). The original ws4kp works out a thickness rounded to half pixels instead, which still leaves uneven lines at such scales.
  - **Vignette**: darker corners, like a picture tube.
  - **Colour fringe on text**: red and blue edges on the text, like a VHS recording, through the shared `text-shadow` mixin (`--fringe-left`, `--fringe-right`).
  - **Colour fringe on pictures**: the red and blue channels of the pictures pulled apart by an SVG filter in `index.ejs` (`#vhs-rgb`). Not on the radar, which animates.
- **Music**: the page plays none. On the TV the Pi plays its own background music (outside this repository). The Webamp (Winamp) player of the original project was removed in 1.9.0.
- **Debug mode** (*Debug mode*, always the last option of the list, for the owner only): the version and the date of the commit it was built from (Lisbon time) are shown small in the bottom right corner, a little in from the edges so the TV's overscan does not cut them, to check that a screen runs the latest version; the track playing on the Pi follows on the same line (it reads `/musica.json` from the Pi service every 15 seconds, the name written by `registo.lua` in `/var/lib/skypaddock-musica/agora.txt`; anywhere but the Pi it stays empty); and the **Status** screen joins the rotation. On by default in the TV command. The date comes from `git log` when building (`vite.config.mjs`); without git it is the build date. Until 1.22.5 these were two options, *Show Version Mark* and *Show Music Track Name*.
- **Status** (Pi, Debug mode only): one card per source of the Pi service (flights, ADS-B receiver and network, OpenSky with its credits, METAR, Verstappen Jet, news, GT World, Plex, work schedule, the TV page itself) with a coloured dot (green fresh, yellow older than the source's usual interval, red much older or `no signal` if never), how long ago it last answered and a short note such as a quota or an error. A four-row card at the bottom right shows the Pi itself (CPU load average (1 minute) and temperature in the colours of the DietPi banner: cyan under 40 C, green from 40, yellow from 50, orange from 60, red from 70; free memory, free disk, Wi-Fi signal, uptime of the Pi and of the service, the music playing and Dark mode; the dot is amber from 60 C or under 100 MB free and red from 70 C or under 50 MB). One page of 20 seconds, the last screen of the rotation. It asks the Pi service again each time it shows (and every minute until it answers), so the ages are current. It reads `/sources.json`, which costs no outside request. Skipped when Debug mode is off or anywhere but the Pi.
- **Display options**: Kiosk, Enable Scan Lines, Enable Vignette, Enable Colour Fringe on Text (VHS), Enable Colour Fringe on Pictures (VHS), Show Breaking News in the Bottom Line, Widescreen (16:9: the side bands and the bottom line use the whole width), Debug mode (always the last option of the list).
- **Personal Schedule**: On or Off (On by default). Shows the (R) schedule under each day on the Work Schedule screen (see there). In the address: `settings-personalSchedule-select=2` (On) or `1` (Off).
- **Personal Gates**: On or Off (On by default), right under Personal Schedule. On: the Gates page of the Work Schedule screen shows the owner's gates of the next day with gates (today while its flights have not all left), with the date in the title; Off: the gates of everybody that day (see there). In the address: `settings-personalGates-select=2` (On) or `1` (Off).
- **Dark Mode**: Off, On or Auto. The weather screens (Current Conditions, Hourly, Travel, Regional, Extended, Almanac, Radar, Air & Pollen, Hazards, News, the loading list) get near black backgrounds and dark blue boxes, with dim amber instead of the bright orange; the aviation, F1 and GT screens are dark already and do not change. Automatic follows the sun at the chosen place (SunCalc): dark from 10 minutes after the sunset until 10 minutes after the sunrise, checked once a minute; with no sunrise or sunset that day (polar day or night), or with no place yet, the normal screens. The backgrounds are recoloured copies (`images/BackGround*_dark.png`, made with `recolor_theme.py dark`); the colours of the blue boxes are the variables `--rows-1`, `--rows-2`, `--column-header` and `--blue-box` (`_dark-mode.scss`). In the address: `settings-darkMode-select=1`, `2` or `3`.
- **Christmas Effects**: Auto (the default), On or Off. Two still pictures of the owner's, over the page background: a string of lights along the top (`images/christmas/luzes.png`, 1920 x 208; put in 25 px from each side and 10 px down from the top of the page so the TV's overscan does not cut the first and last bulbs) and a pile of snow along the bottom (`images/christmas/neve.png`, edge to edge). The text always lies over them: the header and the bottom text get a layer above the pictures, the countdown bar stays under them (`_christmas.scss`), so the pictures lie over the background and the boxes, and the text over them. Auto shows them from 1 to 25 December, the same days as the `[Christmas]` songs (`christmas.mjs`, dates in `utils/christmas.mjs`). Nothing moves: a first version with an animated GIF was taken out as too heavy for the Pi. In the address: `settings-christmas-select=3` (Auto), `2` (On) or `1` (Off).
- **Halloween Effects**: Auto (the default), On or Off. A still cobweb hangs in each top corner of the page (on the widescreen page in the side bands, where they cover no text; on the 4:3 page they are smaller (90 and 130 px of the 640 px page instead of 130 and 190) and cover part of the header); the TV's overscan cuts a little of the corner itself: on the left a web from a sheet of the owner's (`images/halloween/teia.png`), on the right his web with a spider, skulls and bones (`images/halloween/aranha.png`), both cut and shrunk from his pictures. And now and then, in turn on the left and the right, an angry jack-o'-lantern (the owner's picture, `images/halloween/jackolantern.png`) fades in on the line above the bottom line and flickers (on the 4:3 page over the content, since there are no side bands), 30 px in from the edge of the page so the overscan of the TV (about 25 px at the sides) does not cut it; it may go over the edge of the boxes. All over every screen, text included. Auto shows them on 30 and 31 October, the same days as the `[Halloween]` songs, which then play alone on the Pi (the normal songs stay silent). `halloween.mjs`, `_halloween.scss` and the layout in `utils/halloween.mjs`. Only the jack-o'-lanterns are animated (CSS): until 1.19.0 bats flew and spiders walked across the page, and measured on the Pi they took the Chromium from about 163 % to 241 % of a core and made the Hourly Forecast scroll crawl, so they were taken out. In the address: `settings-halloween-select=3` (Auto), `2` (On) or `1` (Off).
- **Retro effects on the decorations**: the Christmas and Halloween decorations are part of the picture, so the scan lines and the vignette go over them, and the pictures get the colour fringe on pictures.

The settings in the address and the TV command are described under [Settings and the TV address](../README.md#settings-and-the-tv-address) in the README.
